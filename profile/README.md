Rookdex is a free, open-source companion site for GTA VI. Track what you find as you play, in English or Norwegian. It installs like an app, works offline and needs no account.

**Use it:** [rookdex.app](https://rookdex.app) · **Code:** [rookdex/rookdex](https://github.com/rookdex/rookdex)

- Your progress stays on your device. No sign-up, no trackers.
- Ready for launch day: 19 November 2026, PS5 and Xbox Series.
- Found a bug or a wrong fact? [Open an issue](https://github.com/rookdex/rookdex/issues).
